# Bakllavë Home Style

Build a complete, polished, responsive website for a small homemade baklava business called Bakllavë e Shtëpisë.

This business sells only baklava. Nothing else.

The website should feel like a real local family business with a premium presentation. It should not look like a generic AI generated website, a template, a SaaS landing page, or a typical Lovable generated site.

VERY IMPORTANT DESIGN DIRECTION

Do not make the website look AI generated.

Avoid the common AI website look, including:

Huge generic hero text

Random gradient backgrounds

Purple or blue SaaS colors

Excessive rounded cards everywhere

Floating glassmorphism boxes

Generic icon grids

Fake statistics

Fake testimonials

Generic stock photography

Excessive animations

Every section looking like a separate card

Repetitive three column layouts

Overly perfect symmetrical sections

Generic phrases such as "Experience the difference"

Random decorative blobs

Excessive badges and pills

A design that looks like a startup landing page

The website should look like it was designed specifically for a small homemade baklava business in Kosovo.

Use the real product and the real photography as the visual identity.

Keep the design human, warm, elegant, simple, and believable.

I want someone visiting the website to immediately think:

This is a real woman making excellent homemade baklava locally.

Not:

This website was generated by AI.

Use small imperfections and natural layouts where appropriate. Let the photography breathe. Use typography, spacing, texture, product details, and subtle visual elements instead of filling every area with UI components.

Overall feeling

The finished website should feel closer to a professionally designed boutique food brand website than a generic ecommerce template.

Think:

Homemade Kosovo baklava + warm family business + beautiful real food photography + cream and honey colors + elegant typography + very simple ordering.

The visual hierarchy should come from photography, whitespace, typography, and composition.

Do not rely on gradients, excessive cards, fake social proof, or generic UI tricks to make the website look designed.

The product itself should make the website beautiful.

Most importantly, make design decisions that feel specific to Bakllavë e Shtëpisë rather than decisions that could be reused unchanged for any restaurant, bakery, coffee shop, or SaaS business.
